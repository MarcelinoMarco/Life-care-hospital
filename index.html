About us

Life Care Hospital is a leading healthcare institution in Kiwengwa, Zanzibar, delivering reliable, patient-focused care through modern medical infrastructure and internationally qualified professionals.

Trusted by locals and visitors alike, Life Care has become the go-to name for consistent, high-standard medical care on the island.

From our base in Kiwengwa, we extend quality healthcare across Zanzibar — from Pwani Mchangani and Kendwa to Nungwi and Paje.

Combining international medical standards with a deep understanding of the local community, Life Care Hospital is where quality and compassion meet.

Why choose us?

Internationally Qualified Professionals — Our medical team brings global expertise to every patient interaction.

Modern Medical Infrastructure — Equipped with the tools and technology needed for accurate diagnosis and effective treatment.

Multiple Convenient Locations — With partner clinics across Kiwengwa, Pwani Mchangani, Kendwa, Nungwi, and Paje, quality care is never far away.

Trusted by Residents and Tourists — A proven track record of reliable care for the local community and international visitors.
